# 🦅 Falcon Agency — Quick Reference Card

> Print this or keep it handy on your phone

## 💬 Top Commands (By Category)

### 🏥 Health & Compliance
- **"health scan"** — Full site compliance audit.
- **"is this safe: [text]"** — Quick AI safety check on any text.
- **"fix titles"** — Auto-rewrite risky product titles for compliance.
- **"add disclaimer"** — Appends FDA disclaimer to all products.

### 🛒 Store & Inventory
- **"store status"** — Product and API health audit.
- **"inventory check"** — Burn rate & stockout predictions (Pillar 1).
- **"order check"** — Today's sales activity.
- **"revenue report"** — Financial performance vs monthly target.
- **"profit report"** — ROI & Munafa (Profit) calculation.

### 📝 Content & Marketing
- **"blog likh [topic]"** — AI generates a SEO blog draft.
- **"drafts dikhao"** — See pending content queue.
- **"publish karo"** — Move draft to WordPress.
- **"publish live karo"** — Go live instantly.
- **"seo audit"** — Full SEO health analysis.
- **"polish customers"** — Customer recovery (Win-back).

### ⚙️ System Control
- **"help"** — Shows the full command menu.
- **"status"** — Check AI workforce & bridge health.
- **"backup"** — Manually trigger a data snapshot.
